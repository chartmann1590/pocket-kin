# Pocket Kin — IARC content rating questionnaire (copy into Play Console)

App: Pocket Kin (com.pocketkin.game), category Games → Casual / Simulation.
Enter a title, email `support@charleshartmann.com`, phone number for the rating process.

## Questionnaire answers

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the app contain any violent content? | **No** (no realistic violence, no fantasy violence with blood/gore; pets are never harmed — illness is treated and recovers) |
| Does the app contain sexual content or nudity? | **No** |
| Does the app contain language offensive to any group? | **No** |
| Does the app reference or promote illegal drugs? | **No** |
| Does the app contain gambling (real or simulated, incl. loot boxes)? | **No** — no casino imagery, no loot boxes; purchases are fixed-content cosmetic bundles |
| Does the app allow users to interact (chat, messaging, user-generated content sharing)? | **No** — single-player only, no communication features |
| Does the app share user-generated content or personal info publicly? | **No** |
| Does the app contain ads? | **Yes** — via AdMob; child-appropriate settings; no ads during care/milestones/walking |
| Does the app offer purchases of digital goods? | **Yes** — permanent cosmetic bundles via Google Play Billing, parent-gated |
| Is the app intended for children under 13? | **Yes, for a general audience including children** — designed for all ages with family-policy safeguards |
| Does the app collect/transmit personal info from children? | **Only with the safeguards described in the privacy policy** (no chat, no behavioral profiles shared beyond ad-SDK consent settings; steps are optional, on-device, never ad-linked) |
| Does the app display health/fitness data? | **Steps only, optional** — read from Health Connect/on-device sensor for walking milestones; never shown to other users |
| Does the app simulate controlled substances/alcohol/tobacco? | **No** |
| Any horror/fear content? | **No** — cozy, gentle tone throughout |

- **ESRB: E (Everyone)** with Interactive Elements: In-game purchases, Ads, Shares info? No, Users interact? No, Digital purchases.
- **PEGI: 3**, **USK: 0**, and equivalent lowest ratings across all authorities.
