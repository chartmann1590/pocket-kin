Pocket Kin — Delete Your Account

Pocket Kin (package com.pocketkin.game, by Charles Hartmann) can be played with no account at all. An account exists only if you turned on optional cloud saves, which creates a Firebase account (anonymous, or linked to a Google account). Deleting it is permanent and removes:

  • your cloud game save (pet state, inventory, room, discoveries, memories, settings, revision);
  • your purchase receipt records stored for restore/refund handling;
  • your Firebase authentication record (anonymous ID and, if linked, the Google link).

Your local pet on your own device is not deleted — uninstalling the app removes local data. Step counts and watch heart-rate readings are never uploaded, so there is no health data of yours on our servers to delete.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Pocket Kin on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete cloud account.
  3. Complete the parent gate and confirm. The app calls our deleteAccount cloud function, which deletes the cloud save and receipts and then the authentication user, and signs you out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email support@charleshartmann.com with the subject “Pocket Kin account deletion”. If you linked a Google account, send the request from that email address so we can identify the account. We will delete the same data listed above and confirm by email within 30 days (normally much sooner).
